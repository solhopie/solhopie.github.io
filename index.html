About Solhopie

Solhopie is a one-person 3D asset studio focused on the props games actually need: pallets, racks, crates, carts — the industrial backbone of warehouses, factories and logistics sims. Every asset is built procedurally to real-world scale with a clean, shared PBR workflow that keeps your project light. More themed packs are on the way.
